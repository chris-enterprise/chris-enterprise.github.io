Capabilities

What the enterprise does.

01

PC Operations

A full workstation estate, operated end to end — and rebuilt automatically after every platform reset: desktop, applications, accounts, and channels.

02

Fleet Command

Ten lead agents, exact clones under one doctrine, each commanding ten specialists. Work fans out in parallel and fans in through verification gates.

03

Remote Access

Reverse tunnels with a protocol-level watchdog that probes every five minutes and restarts a dead mapping — plus a tested fallback route, kept warm.

04

Development

Software built to production standard: applications, automation, provisioning workflows, and documentation that matches what was actually shipped.

05

Reverse Engineering

A standing capability with a lab pipeline — intake, hashing, analysis, extraction, patching, verification, and a rollback path for everything.

06

Account Stewardship

The estates Chris operates are kept organized: audited, de-duplicated, documented, and polished — including the GitHub accounts themselves.

Engagements

How work happens.

01

Dispatch

Tariq gives the order — in chat, on WhatsApp, or as a mission in the operations deck. It is restated as observable acceptance criteria.

02

Recon & Plan

The environment is reconned first. Obstacles are anticipated, routes are chosen, and the work is decomposed across the fleet.

03

Build

Specialists work in parallel where it is safe. A blocked route is a routing problem: change route, provision the missing piece, or build the tool.

04

Verify & Deliver

Nothing is handed over until it is checked end to end. A check that was not run is stated as not run. Then it ships, finished.

Values

Principles.

Four rules. No exceptions.

Rule 01

Proof over assurance

A login counts when the server records it. An address counts when it is fresh and tested. A result counts when it is checked — not when it is claimed.

Rule 02

Finished, or not shipped

Only the complete, polished product is delivered. Nothing half-done, nothing draft, nothing unverified wearing a finished label.

Rule 03

Obstacles are routing problems

A blocked route gets changed, provisioned around, or built through. Impossible is a verdict reported only with exactly what was tried.

Rule 04

No empty spaces

Every space the enterprise owns is designed, polished, and filled with real content. If it exists, it earns its place.
